01 / Our Origin

Considered origins,
enduring impact.

The Deco House emerged from a fundamental desire to simplify the specification process for architects and designers. We observed a fragmented marketplace and responded by creating a curated sanctuary of materials.

Every stone, natural veneer, engineered composite, and acoustic panel in our collection is rigorously evaluated not just for its immediate aesthetic appeal, but for how it responds to natural light, daily touch, and the passage of time.
